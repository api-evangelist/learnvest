# LearnVest

> LearnVest is a discontinued US personal-finance company. Founded by Alexa von Tobel and backed by Accel, it sold subscription financial planning pairing consumers with CFP professionals alongside budgeting and goal-tracking tools. Northwestern Mutual acquired LearnVest in 2015 and shut the planning service down on 2018-06-05. learnvest.com survives only as an archived consumer financial-education article site operated under the Northwestern Mutual brand. LearnVest never published a public API, developer portal, or SDK.

## Status

- Product status: discontinued (planning service ended 2018-06-05)
- Owner: The Northwestern Mutual Life Insurance Company
- API surface: none — no developer portal, documentation, OpenAPI, sandbox, changelog, status page, or /.well-known/ discovery
- Public packages: none on npm or PyPI
- GitHub: https://github.com/LearnVest (organization exists, zero public repositories)
